# DocNic Funnel V1

Private conversion-funnel concept / mini-audit demo for DocNic Cosmetic Surgery Clinic.

## Purpose

This is a prospect-facing funnel concept designed to demonstrate how a consultation-first journey could reduce friction between interest and consultation.

## V1 structure

1. Hero / positioning
2. Goal-based procedure discovery
3. DocNic approach
4. Clinical leadership
5. Consultation journey
6. Patient testimonial proof
7. FAQ
8. Consultation CTA

## Claim discipline

Clinic-specific facts and figures are based on publicly available information from DocNic's official website. No pricing, guarantees, fabricated reviews, or unsupported medical claims are introduced.

## Important

This is a demonstration concept and is not the official DocNic website. The CTA links visitors to DocNic's official contact page rather than collecting medical information on this demo.
